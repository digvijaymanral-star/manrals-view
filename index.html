THE STORY BEHIND MANRAL'S VIEW

Travel changed the way I see the world.

I started travelling when I was 17. Along the way, I discovered that the real value of travel isn't simply reaching another destination. It's the exposure — the people you meet, the conversations you have, the friendships you make and the experiences you carry home.

But modern travel can sometimes become shallow: book a hotel, visit the popular sights, take a few photographs and leave without ever understanding how people actually live there.

Manral's View is my attempt to offer something different: experiences that let travellers participate in the life of a place rather than simply pass through it.
