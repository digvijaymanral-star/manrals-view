THE STORY BEHIND MANRAL'S VIEW

Hi, I'm Digvijay.

I started travelling when I was 17, and since then travel has been one of my biggest teachers.

It gave me exposure, new perspectives, new people, new friendships and experiences that stayed long after I returned home.

But I also noticed how easy it has become to travel without really experiencing a place — book a hotel, see the popular sights, take a few photographs and leave.

Manral's View is my way of sharing a different kind of journey: one where you meet people, participate in everyday life and leave with a story of your own.

— Digvijay Manral
